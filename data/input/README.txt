Put your test videos in this folder.

Supported: .mp4  .avi  .mov  .mkv

They will appear behind "Open Video..." on the Cameras screen, and can be
wired to a named camera in config/cameras.yaml.

Build the bundled synthetic demo clip with:

    python scripts/make_demo_video.py

See docs/DATA.md for where to obtain footage you can legitimately use, and
which scenarios are worth recording.


==========================================================================
WHAT IS IN HERE, AND WHAT MAY BE SUBMITTED
==========================================================================

Provenance matters more than picture quality. A clip that cannot be
explained is worse than no clip. Three categories:

CLEARED FOR THE SUBMISSION
--------------------------
  fence_own.mp4
      Filmed by the team on an iPhone 16 from a fixed position. Two people,
      a chain-link railing in the foreground and a shade-net fence behind
      it. PERSON #02 vaults the railing at about frame 144. This is the
      clip the fence-relationship work is built and verified against.
      Wired to CAM-01.

  demo_border.mp4, sample_02.mp4
      Synthetic, generated by scripts/make_demo_video.py and
      scripts/make_perimeter_video.py. Ours, so no licensing question, but
      say plainly that they are synthetic if they appear anywhere.

EVALUATION ONLY - MUST NOT BE SUBMITTED
---------------------------------------
Fine for developing and testing against. Not for the demo video, the slide
deck, the report, or anything handed in.

  WhatsApp Video 2026-09-08 at 20.13.37.mp4    Getty preview 1219650634
  WhatsApp Video 2026-09-08 at 20.12.55.mp4    Getty preview 2075325506
      Getty Images preview files. The watermark and asset id are burned
      into the picture. A preview file is licensed for evaluation, which is
      what this is. Replace with your own footage before submitting.

  img_3894_eval.mp4
      A phone recording of a SCREEN playing film footage, not camera
      footage of a real place. Measurable signs: moire crosshatch across
      flat areas, which only appears when a camera photographs a display;
      black surround with the panel edge visible; three shot cuts at 2.8s,
      7.0s and 10.3s, which a fixed camera cannot produce; and war-film
      content. Somebody else's film. Wired to CAM-02 with NO geometry,
      because a scene that cuts three times has no fixed viewpoint for a
      polygon to describe.

      Measured behaviour of the pipeline on it: 2 people on screen become
      6 track ids, because every cut ends the identities and starts new
      ones. Pose runs and produces 548 readings, but NONE of them contain
      ankle keypoints, so no standing height can be measured, so the
      movement layer reports "no reliable standing height yet" and every
      behaviour stays UNKNOWN. That is the system declining to guess, and
      it is the correct answer for torso-only close-ups.

  WhatsApp Video 2026-09-08 at 19.56.37.mp4
  WhatsApp Video 2026-09-08 at 19.57.08.mp4
      Unwired. Provenance not established.

WHAT IS STILL MISSING
---------------------
Own-filmed footage of SEVERAL people approaching a boundary in a low or
crouched posture, from a fixed camera. About 20 seconds, three or four
people, moving across the view rather than along the camera axis, with one

multi-person tracking, crouched approach, boundary interaction and a
